About Peter

Fully qualified. Properly trained. Genuinely keen.

Hello, I'm Peter. Your new local electrician. I finished my apprenticeship with C.J. Ryder Lawlor Ltd. and set up Always On Electrical for jobs on weekends and evening callouts.
