Rocker

A wall rocker switch, face on: the O is split into two paddles and the top one is the indicator lamp. Off: dark. On: amber. The most physical and the most “electrician” of the three. It is also the heaviest O, the change is colour only (so the least kinetic), and it is the least literal at 16 px.
